Write `!` before the field's type, as in `pointX :: !Int` or `Point !Int !Int`.
If the module has StrictData on, the field is lazy because of its `~`: delete the `~` instead.
Keep the field lazy only if something needs it to be, such as a value defined in terms of itself, or one that is expensive and often never read.
In that case, suppress the finding and say in the suppression what needs the field to be lazy.
