A Validity instance that writes out validate and does not start from genericValidate checks less than it looks like it does: the fields' own invariants go unchecked unless every one of them is remembered by hand, and a field added later is forgotten silently.
Starting from genericValidate keeps every field checked however the type changes, so what is written after it is only what is special about this type.
One shape for every instance also means a reader sees at a glance where the structural check ends and the type's own checks begin.
